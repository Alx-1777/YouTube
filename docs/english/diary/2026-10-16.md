## 要改的

| 你说的 | 更好的说法 | 要点 |
|---|---|---|
| I just **so very sleepy** | I **was so sleepy** | 少了动词 was |
| I can't just cannot focus on myself on studying | I **couldn't focus on studying** | 过去的事用 couldn't；focus on 后面直接接事 |
| my English is **being** more and more fluent | my English is **getting** more and more fluent | "变得"用 get |
| I wanted the DMV to the cat, **took the task** | I **went to** the DMV, **took the test**, and got my permit | test /test/ 和 task /tæsk/ 不一样 |
| I'll just **run** the Tesla Model Y | I'll just **rent** a Tesla Model Y | rent /rent/ 是"租"，n 和 t 要发出来 |
| moving to the other city, the other states | I **might move to another city or state** | another 而不是 the other |
| today I **eat** very healthy | today I **ate really healthy** / **ate clean** | 已经发生的事用 ate |
| **in the for** lunch | **for** lunch | |
| pure natural food instead of any like cakes or sugars | **whole foods, no cake or sugar** | whole foods = 天然、没加工的食物 |
| I decided just not eat anything | I decided **not to eat anything else** today | decide **not to** do |
