## 做得好的
- 第一天就发出去了，而且是在真实场景里边走边说。

## 要改的（按重要性）

| 你说的 | 更好的说法 | 要点 |
|---|---|---|
| I asked Claude **that how can I** improve my English | I asked Claude **how I can** improve my English | **间接疑问句用陈述语序**，也不加 that。今天最重要的一条 |
| I'm gonna **pose** a short | I'm gonna **post** a Short | post 结尾的 /st/ 要发出来 |
| **As an international student, my English** is not very good | **I'm an international student, and** my English isn't great yet | 原句主语变成了 my English，像在说"英语是留学生" |
| I just went **in** the gym yesterday | I just went **to** the gym yesterday | go **to** the gym |
| Today I **have** classes（晚上说的） | Today I **had** classes | 已经发生的事用过去时 |
| I am **going to going** back to my dorm | I'm **heading back** to my dorm | 两个 going 撞车；heading back 更地道 |
| I **do** this just **in order to** practice | I'm **doing** this to practice my English | 正在进行的计划用进行时；just in order to 太啰嗦 |
| YouTube Shorts **as I can only upload video about three minutes** | YouTube Shorts **can only be up to three minutes long** | 用 Shorts 当主语，句子就顺了 |
| Let me show you the street **a lot of cars** | Let me show you the street. **There are a lot of cars** right now | 两句话要断开 |
| Claude **what** analyze this video and tell me the **grammar problems** | Claude **will** analyze this video and point out my **grammar mistakes** | 说错叫 mistakes；problems 也行但不常用 |
| it is 8 a.m., **or no**, 8 p.m. | it's 8 a.m., **I mean**, 8 p.m. | 口误纠正用 **I mean** |
