## 要改的

| 你说的 | 更好的说法 | 要点 |
|---|---|---|
| Day 3 of **practice in** my English | Day 3 of **practicing** my English | of 后面用 -ing |
| Today, I don't think, not very special | **Today wasn't very special**, but... | 直接说 Today wasn't... |
| in AIR we should focus on... | **in the AI era**, we should focus on... | era 读 /ˈɪrə/，前面加 the |
| the **mechanics** that something that AI cannot replace | **mechanical things / hardware** that AI can't replace | mechanics 是"力学"或"机修工" |
| maybe **I** will replace this too | maybe **AI** will replace this too | 转写成了 I：**A-I** 两个字母要读清楚 /eɪ aɪ/ |
| a lot of people lose their **job** | a lot of people lose their **jobs** | 很多人，jobs 用复数 |
| So that's the full point today | **That's my main point for today.** | |
| I thought I just eat more to help me focus | **I thought eating more would help me focus**, but I was wrong. | 过去的想法用 would |
| Control my mouse（管住嘴） | **I need to watch what I eat.** / **I need to cut back.** | "管住嘴"不能直译，mouse 是鼠标，嘴是 mouth |

## 今日表达
1. **in the AI era**（在 AI 时代）
2. **New opportunities will emerge.**（新的机会会出现。）
