## 要改的

| 你说的 | 更好的说法 | 要点 |
|---|---|---|
| I read a book from 0 to 1 by ... | I read a book called **Zero to One** by **Peter Thiel** | 书名前用 **called**；作者名要说清楚（Thiel 读 /tiːl/） |
| I learned a very interesting **book** that is... | I learned a very interesting **idea** from it: | 学到的是 idea，不是 book |
| the perspective of future | the perspective of **the** future | future 前要加 the |
| What do you think you can actually decide with the amount of money you make? | **What do you think actually decides how much money you make?** | 主语是"什么"，不是"你" |
| to be **specifically** | to be **specific** | 固定说法：to be specific |
| we have to predict the **past** from the perspective of future | we have to look at **today** from the perspective of the future | 想说的是"现在"，不是"过去" |
| no one is making, but it is usually... | something no one is making, **but people need** | 这句没说完，补上"但人们需要" |
| I think in a very, very good viewpoint, it just changed my mind | **I think it's a really good point. It changed the way I think.** | change my mind 是"改变主意"；"改变思维方式"用 change the way I think |
| it is basically today and YouTube is up to three minutes | **That's it for today. Shorts can only be up to three minutes.** | 结束语用 That's it for today |

**发音**：开头那句转写成了 "**sinking** barriers"，你想说的应该是 "**thinking** barriers"。th /θ/ 要把舌尖放在上下齿之间吹气，不是 s。
