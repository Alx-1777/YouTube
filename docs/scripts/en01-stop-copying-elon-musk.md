## 稿子

**[Hook, 0:00]**
**I used to copy everything Elon Musk did.**
He played video games, so I played the same games. He learned to code, so I learned to code.
Then I read a book that told me: **stop copying him.**
（开口第一句就是这句，不要先说 hello。）

**[The book, 0:15]**
The book is called ***Zero to One***, by **Peter Thiel**.
He co-founded PayPal with Elon Musk.
（Thiel 读 /tiːl/，像 "teal"。）

**[Idea 1: 0 to 1 vs 1 to n, 0:30]**
Thiel says there are two kinds of progress.
**Copying something that already works is "one to n."**
**Creating something completely new is "zero to one."**
Most people only do one to n. They copy.

**[Idea 2: Competition, 0:55]**
His second idea surprised me: **competition is for losers.**
If everyone does the same thing, nobody makes money.
For example, airlines move millions of people, but they make **about 37 cents** per passenger.
Google has almost no real competitor, so it makes a lot more.

**[My mistake, 1:25]**
So here's what I got wrong.
**The next Elon Musk won't build rockets. Elon already did that.**
If I copy his past, I'm just doing "one to n."

**[What I'll do instead, 1:45]**
Now I ask myself a different question:
**What will people need in the future, that nobody is building yet?**
Especially in the AI era, the old paths are already taken.

**[Ending + question, 2:05]**
Thiel has one question I love:
**"What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"**
**Tell me your answer in the comments.**
I'm a Chinese student in the US, practicing my English every day. See you tomorrow.
